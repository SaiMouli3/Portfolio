# MASTER CINEMATIC VIDEO PROMPT — THE JOURNEY OF AN ENGINEER

Create a premium, emotionally engaging, stylized 3D animated short film showing the life journey of one Indian boy growing from childhood into a 23-year-old software engineer and AI researcher.

The entire film must feel like one continuous cinematic story, produced by a world-class animation studio.

FORMAT:
- Cinematic widescreen 16:9
- Premium stylized 3D animated feature-film aesthetic
- Beautiful environmental storytelling
- Physically realistic lighting and shadows
- Natural character animation
- Smooth cinematic camera movements
- Emotional storytelling
- High-quality materials, textures and details
- Consistent protagonist identity throughout

IMPORTANT CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:

There is only ONE main protagonist.

Use the supplied character references as the identity source.

Preserve his recognizable eyes, eyebrows, nose, skin tone, hairstyle and facial identity throughout the story.

His appearance must evolve naturally with age:
- Childhood: 8-year-old Indian schoolboy.
- College: 20–21-year-old Indian engineering student.
- Present: 23-year-old Indian software and AI engineer.

Never randomly change his face, ethnicity, hairstyle or physical identity.

---

SCENE 1 — THE BEGINNING: CHILDHOOD

Duration: approximately 6 seconds.

Open with a beautiful cinematic wide shot of a peaceful Indian residential neighborhood in the early morning.

Golden sunlight passes through trees. Birds fly in the distance. A young Indian schoolboy walks towards school carrying his navy-blue backpack.

He wears a light-blue school shirt, navy trousers and black school shoes.

Camera follows him from behind with a smooth cinematic tracking movement.

His backpack gently moves as he walks. Trees sway in the morning breeze.

The atmosphere should feel innocent, nostalgic and full of possibility.

Transition: The camera follows the boy towards his home after school.

SCENE 2 — THE FIRST LOVE: VIDEO GAMES

Duration: approximately 6 seconds.

Show the same 8-year-old boy sitting at his computer desk in his cozy childhood bedroom.

He is enthusiastically playing video games.

The monitor casts a beautiful blue glow on his face.

His fingers move naturally on the keyboard and mouse. His eyes follow the screen. He smiles with excitement.

Camera slowly pushes towards his face, then moves towards the computer monitor.

The colorful gaming screen fills the entire frame.

TRANSITION:

The gaming visuals gradually transform into programming code.

The colorful screen becomes a professional coding interface.

Use this as a visual metaphor for the beginning of his interest in technology.

SCENE 3 — ENGINEERING COLLEGE

Duration: approximately 7 seconds.

Reveal the older 20-year-old version of the same protagonist.

He is now walking through a beautiful Indian engineering college campus carrying his backpack.

Show academic buildings, trees, pathways and students in the distant background.

Use a smooth cinematic rear tracking shot.

Transition into a computer science laboratory.

Show him sitting at a computer, seriously learning programming.

He types on his laptop, studies the screen, pauses to think and continues coding.

Camera slowly moves from an over-the-shoulder view to a three-quarter view of his focused face.

The mood should communicate curiosity, ambition and the transformation from a child who loved computers into a student who wants to understand them.

SCENE 4 — GRADUATION: A MILESTONE

Duration: approximately 6 seconds.

Show the same protagonist at age 23, wearing a black graduation gown on his university campus.

He holds his degree certificate and looks towards the university building with a proud, emotional smile.

Golden-hour sunlight illuminates the scene.

The camera slowly pulls backwards and upwards, revealing the university architecture.

The graduation gown moves gently in the breeze.

The emotional feeling should be achievement, gratitude and the beginning of a new chapter.

TRANSITION:

The university building gradually transforms into a sophisticated modern corporate office building.

Use a seamless architectural match transition.

SCENE 5 — PROFESSIONAL LIFE: SOFTWARE ENGINEER

Duration: approximately 7 seconds.

Reveal the same 23-year-old protagonist working in a sophisticated modern corporate office in Bengaluru.

He is a professional software engineer working at NatWest.

He wears smart casual professional clothing.

Show him sitting at his workstation with dual monitors, typing code and studying technical information.

The office should have modern glass architecture, sophisticated workstations and natural daylight.

Camera slowly moves towards him from an over-the-shoulder angle.

Show his focused expression and natural interaction with the computer.

Communicate the feeling of achieving a professional milestone and entering the real engineering world.

Do not generate fake company logos or illegible company branding.

TRANSITION:

The camera slowly pushes towards his computer monitor.

Programming code transforms into neural network visualizations and mathematical diagrams.

The office environment gradually transitions into a personal late-night AI research workstation.

SCENE 6 — BEYOND THE JOB: AI AND RESEARCH

Duration: approximately 8 seconds.

Show the same protagonist working late at night at his personal engineering workstation.

This is no longer the corporate office.

His workspace contains:
- Multiple monitors.
- Powerful GPU workstation.
- Mechanical keyboard.
- Technical books.
- Research notebooks.
- Computer science diagrams.

His monitors display sophisticated technical visualizations:

- Transformer architecture.
- Attention mechanisms.
- Neural networks.
- Large language model diagrams.
- GPU architecture.
- Machine learning training.
- Mathematical equations.
- Programming code.

He types code, pauses to think, studies a technical diagram and continues experimenting.

Camera slowly moves around his workstation, revealing the different monitors and finally returning to his focused face.

Blue monitor illumination mixes with warm desk lighting.

The mood should communicate curiosity, deep learning, experimentation and a desire to understand technology at a fundamental level.

SCENE 7 — THE BUILDER: THE NEXT CHAPTER

Duration: approximately 6 seconds.

Show the protagonist standing in front of his workstation.

The camera slowly moves towards him.

His monitors show different engineering projects, AI experiments and technical visualizations.

He looks towards the screen with confidence and curiosity.

The camera moves past him towards the computer monitor.

The monitor gradually fills the entire frame.

The final frame becomes a clean, dark cinematic background with subtle particles and soft lighting.

Leave clean negative space in the centre for the website to display:

"THE JOURNEY CONTINUES."

Then transition into the next section of the portfolio website.

GLOBAL CINEMATIC DIRECTION

The complete film must have:

1. Consistent character identity across all ages.
2. Natural and believable character movement.
3. Smooth camera tracking, dolly, push-in and pull-back movements.
4. Beautiful cinematic environmental lighting.
5. Carefully designed transitions between chapters.
6. Emotional progression from childhood innocence to professional ambition.
7. Premium stylized 3D animation quality.
8. No random character changes.
9. No distorted hands or faces.
10. No unnecessary futuristic holograms or exaggerated sci-fi effects.

IMPORTANT STORYTELLING PRINCIPLE:

This is not a random collection of animated scenes.

It is one person's journey.

A child who loved computers grows into an engineering student, becomes a professional software engineer and continues exploring artificial intelligence, research and technology.

The final feeling should be:

"He didn't stop learning when he got the job. That was only the beginning."
